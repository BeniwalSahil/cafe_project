We created SKB as a soft landing in the middle of Gurgaon’s fast-moving days. It’s a place for the first coffee before work, a lunch that becomes a long conversation, and one more cup before heading home.

Everything we serve has a purpose: bright, carefully brewed coffee; food with honest ingredients; and a room that gives you permission to slow down.
